# Grade risk with confidence bounds and evidence gates

For one- through four-week horizons, grading uses directional 95% upper risk bounds rather than point estimates. As clarified by ADR 0016, these are one-sided bounds rather than the upper endpoint of a two-sided 95% interval. Conservative requires expiration breach at or below 0.5% and path touch at or below 1%; Safe permits up to 2% and 5% respectively. Aggressive is a user-configurable tier, defaulting to 5% and 10%, and must be no stricter than Safe. Exceeding the configured Aggressive limits is Dangerous. Fewer than 100 effective independent paths yields Insufficient Evidence instead of a grade, and five- through eight-week scenario horizons are never graded. Effective overrides are recorded in the model key and export.
