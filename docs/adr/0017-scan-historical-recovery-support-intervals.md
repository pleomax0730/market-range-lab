# Scan historical recovery support intervals without recommending strikes

For a downside Put candidate, the application scans a continuous moneyness grid and identifies every contiguous price interval whose conditional historical recovery estimate, Greenwood-type 95% lower confidence bound, and effective assignment-event count meet explicit thresholds for a selected deadline. Daily History supports 7/14/21/30 trading sessions; Weekly-Only History supports 1/2/3/4 weeks. Recovery can target the mapped strike or a break-even level formed by applying the current candidate's net Premium/strike ratio to each scanned historical strike.

The scan uses the same full-history, same-session raw paths and close-based assignment/recovery definitions as candidate recovery. Right-censored observations remain in the Kaplan–Meier risk set. The default grid is 70%–100% of the Reference Price at 0.5% increments, with 75% recovery, a 60% lower confidence bound, and 20 effective assignment events required. Thresholds and the grid are user-visible and exported. When no point qualifies, the application reports no Historical Recovery Support Interval and may identify the closest point, but it never relaxes thresholds automatically. The result is historical support evidence, not an option-chain lookup, recommended strike, or recovery guarantee.
